## 1. Executive Understanding

Harlink Investment Ltd. is a Nigeria-based, **export-focused trade facilitation and agency services company**. Its primary function is to help businesses move commodities from Nigeria into international markets by coordinating the administrative, documentation, regulatory, clearance, and operational work associated with exports.

Harlink serves businesses that already produce, purchase, or trade export commodities. Instead of making those companies navigate complex local export processes alone, Harlink coordinates the relevant steps and interfaces with the appropriate bodies and service providers.

**Harlink's principal earnings come from agency fees** charged for carrying out export-related work, obtaining and assembling the required documentation, and coordinating necessary processes. Harlink also **owns trucks** and undertakes trucking/haulage work. It **does not own a warehouse**; warehousing is delivered through partnerships. Olam was named in the discussion as a relevant commercial relationship, especially for commodities such as **cocoa and cashew**; the precise nature of the Olam relationship and any permission to reference Olam publicly must be verified.

Harlink also provides **export advisory/consulting**, including guidance about commodities and prospective export destinations. **It does not directly find overseas buyers for customers as a core service.** Online platforms for discovering buyers exist, but their availability should not be represented as a buyer-matching service supplied by Harlink.

### One-sentence positioning

> Harlink is an export-focused trade facilitation and logistics partner that helps Nigerian commodity businesses manage export documentation, regulatory procedures, customs-related processes, and cargo movement.

### Central value proposition

> **We handle the complexities of exporting so businesses can focus on their commodities and markets.**

This is proposed marketing language, not a claim that Harlink can bypass regulatory requirements or guarantee shipment timelines.

## 3. Business Model: How Harlink Creates Value

### Primary offering — export agency and facilitation

Harlink takes responsibility for coordinating the practical and administrative work required for a customer's export operation, within the scope agreed with the customer. This may involve:

- Determining the procedural and documentation requirements for a shipment.
- Preparing, gathering, checking, submitting, and following up on export-related paperwork, as applicable.
- Coordinating with customs, port, regulatory, and inspection stakeholders as required.
- Organising inspections, clearances, and required steps, subject to agency rules and approvals.
- Helping move goods through local logistics and terminal arrangements.
- Keeping the customer informed of requirements, outstanding steps, and export progress.

**Commercial logic:** The client pays Harlink an **agency/service fee** for Harlink's expertise, coordination, administrative execution, and handling of export formalities. Statutory fees, third-party expenses, haulage, and storage charges should not automatically be presented as agency-fee revenue.

### Supporting offering — owned-fleet trucking and haulage

Harlink has its **own trucks**. Its transport capability supports export operations by moving commodities, containers, or cargo between relevant locations as contracted. Do not assert fleet size, truck specifications, nationwide coverage, or guaranteed delivery speeds until confirmed.

### Supporting offering — partner-based warehousing

Harlink can **arrange warehousing through business partners** but does **not own its own warehouse**. Website wording must make this distinction clear:

- **Use:** “Warehousing solutions through trusted partners” or “We coordinate warehousing and storage as part of the export workflow.”
- **Avoid:** “Our warehouses,” “our storage facilities,” or claims of warehouse ownership.

### Advisory offering — export consulting

Harlink can advise customers on export readiness, commodity considerations, possible export markets, and the procedures likely to apply. Clarify the actual depth of this consulting with management before suggesting specialised research, guaranteed market access, or product development services.

### Buyer sourcing boundary

Harlink **does not directly match clients with international buyers** as a core offering. The website should not promise buyers, sales contracts, overseas distribution, or guaranteed export orders.

## 5. Regulatory & Industry Ecosystem

Harlink's service depends on navigating Nigerian export processes and dealing with relevant institutions. **Working with an agency is not the same as being accredited by it.** The final website must distinguish licenses/registrations held, professional memberships, and organisations Harlink routinely engages.

| Body | Full name / relevance | Website claim status |
|---|---|---|
| NCS | Nigeria Customs Service — customs processing and clearance | Confirm exact licence/agent status |
| NPA | Nigerian Ports Authority — port-related operations | Clarify operational relationship |
| NIMASA | Nigerian Maritime Administration and Safety Agency — maritime regulatory ecosystem | Clarify relevance; not automatic accreditation |
| NSC | Nigerian Shippers' Council — shipper/port-services ecosystem | Clarify relevance |
| NEPC | Nigerian Export Promotion Council — export promotion and exporter-related processes | Confirm actual registration/certificate |
| CRFFN | Council for the Regulation of Freight Forwarding in Nigeria — professional/regulatory body for freight forwarding | Confirm registration/status |
| NDLEA | National Drug Law Enforcement Agency — enforcement and applicable inspection requirements | Do not imply NDLEA “approval” without evidence |
| Agricultural / health / inspection bodies | May apply depending on product, destination, and export rules | Identify which actually apply to Harlink's shipments |

**Unclear term:** “Nasima” was mentioned alongside NIMASA. It may refer to another body or be a misstatement. Verify before using it in public copy.

**Important website rule:** Never imply that Harlink's relationships allow it to skip inspections, bypass rules, secure automatic approvals, or guarantee faster customs clearance. A safer and stronger message is that Harlink's experience helps clients **prepare correctly, coordinate efficiently, and avoid unnecessary procedural delays**, while all approvals remain subject to the responsible authorities.

**Evidence to request:** Copies of current licences, registrations, certificates, memberships, or authorisations; issuing body; certificate number where appropriate; expiration date; and permission to display on the website.

## 6. Illustrative Export Workflow

This is a **conceptual example** for explaining Harlink's role. It is not a verified standard operating procedure for every shipment.

1. **Client enquiry and scope:** A commodity exporter contacts Harlink about a shipment, goods, destination, and required support.
2. **Advisory and requirements:** Harlink determines which export activities and documents are relevant to the assignment.
3. **Documentation and compliance coordination:** Harlink gathers and follows up on the required documents and relevant checks.
4. **Cargo preparation and storage:** Where required, Harlink coordinates partner-based warehousing and cargo handling.
5. **Ground transport:** Harlink may deploy its trucks for agreed movement of goods or containers.
6. **EPT / port-related coordination:** Harlink supports the necessary export-processing, customs, and logistics interfaces.
7. **Shipment handover / follow-through:** Harlink coordinates with the relevant shipment parties according to the scope of engagement.

Do not claim that Harlink itself operates an EPT unless management confirms ownership or an operating licence. “EPT” should be expanded in the website as **Export Processing Terminal** where appropriate, and the exact service Harlink provides should be stated.
